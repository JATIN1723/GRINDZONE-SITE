A gym built for people who show up

The Grindzone Fitness is a training space in Umela, Naigaon West, run by its founder, Shivananda Behera. The floor is black, the equipment is red and black, and the focus is simple: turn up, do the work, and keep doing it.

Whether you want to lose weight, build muscle or just get moving again, the coaches here work with you on form, routine and progress. Real change comes from consistency, and having people around you who push you to stay consistent makes it easier.

  • Healthy living, built one session at a time
  • Supportive coaching for every fitness level
  • A friendly community that keeps you accountable
